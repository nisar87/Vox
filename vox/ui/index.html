      Hearing

      Automatic uses your AI provider to understand speech, which copes far better with accents and names. "On this PC only" works offline and keeps audio on the machine, but mishears more.

      Live streams your voice to Gemini while you are in a conversation, so it hears you directly. Use a headset: with loudspeakers Vox can hear itself.
      Works with the window closed. With "Accurate", every stretch of speech the microphone picks up while standby is on is sent to your AI provider to check for "Hi Vox". With "Private", nothing leaves the PC, but accented speech is often missed. Either way, anything not starting with "Hi Vox" is ignored.
      If "Hi Vox" is not picked up, press the button and say "Hi Vox". Repeat three or four times. Vox learns how your voice sounds to it.
      If Vox acts before you finish a sentence, choose a more patient setting.
